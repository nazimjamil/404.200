Bio — LongRev. 04.2026

I am a Principal Software Engineer at Expedia Group, and have been in the building for nine years. Eighteen years total in the industry. Full-stack by practice, with a deliberate focus on user experience as craft.

My remit cuts across four engineering organisations — FinTech, Data, ML, and Web Experiences — as the architectural bridge between teams, geographies, and domains. The work is the seam between systems: where the GraphQL contract meets the booking funnel, where the agent meets the legacy service, where the on-call meets the post-incident review.

I chose to return to IC at Principal level over a Director promotion — believing the highest-leverage role was shaping long-term technical strategy and architecture, not running the org chart that ships it.

I architected Phoenix.ai, Expedia's flagship internal AI product. Before that, the internal developer platform backbone, and the GraphQL experience API that unified data access and server-side localisation at global scale.

My conviction is simple. Reliability is an engineering discipline, not a reactive one. Observability is not a dashboard you add later — it is a product surface, designed. On-call is design feedback. The post-mortem is the document. Write it first.

Outside the building: writing about agentic systems, advising founders on platform architecture, and reading more than I publish.
